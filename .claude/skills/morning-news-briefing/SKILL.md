Search the web for today's top world/general news headlines. Use WebSearch to find current, significant stories from the past 24 hours across major topics: world affairs, politics, notable events. Compile a concise briefing (5-8 stories) with a one or two sentence summary each, in plain prose (no heavy bullet formatting unless it aids clarity). Include source names. Keep the whole briefing skimmable in under 2 minutes of reading. Present it directly in the response — no file needed.
